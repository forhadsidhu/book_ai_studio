BookAI Studio — Offline AI Book Writing, Images, Audio & Video Desktop App

Write entire books on your own computer — no monthly fees, no cloud, no data leaving your machine. BookAI Studio is a cross-platform desktop app that turns your ideas into fully-illustrated, narrated books using local AI models. Generate chapters, manage characters, create scene artwork, export audiobooks, and assemble a book trailer — all from a single clean interface.

Perfect for indie authors, hobbyist novelists, content creators, self-publishers and worldbuilders who want the power of AI writing tools without subscription costs, word-count caps, or content restrictions.

Key Features

  • AI Chapter Generation — Produce full chapters in seconds using your own locally-hosted language model via LM Studio. Continue from where you left off or start fresh.
  • Character Profile Manager — Add characters manually or let the AI extract them from your draft. Auto-generate portraits, backstories, and descriptions.
  • Scene Image & Cover Art — Generate illustrations for every chapter using local Stable Diffusion, AUTOMATIC1111, Pollinations, or DALL·E. Multiple visual styles supported.
  • Audio Narration — Convert any chapter to natural-sounding speech with adjustable voice, speed, and engine. Export full audiobooks ready for distribution.
  • Book Trailer Video — Automatically stitch generated scene images into a polished MP4 trailer to promote your book.
  • Writing Styles — Choose from presets like Dark Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Romance, Thriller, Literary, Horror and more — each tuned to produce distinct prose.
  • Book Import — Bring in an existing manuscript from .docx, .txt, or .pdf and keep writing with AI assistance.
  • Plot Suggestions — Get context-aware story ideas from the built-in AI Assistant panel whenever you hit a wall.
  • 100% Offline & Private — All generation runs on your hardware. Your manuscript never touches a third-party server.

What's Inside

  • Five integrated workspaces: Write, Characters, Images, Audio, and Video
  • Chapter-based project structure with auto-save
  • AI Assistant side panel with plot suggestions
  • Configurable max words per generation (up to thousands of words per call)
  • Exports to .docx, .mp3/.wav, .png, and .mp4

Supported AI Backends

  • Text: LM Studio (local, free) — compatible with any GGUF model such as Llama, Mistral, Qwen, Gemma
  • Images: Local Diffusers, AUTOMATIC1111 WebUI, Pollinations (no GPU required), OpenAI DALL·E
  • Audio: pyttsx3 (fully offline), with configurable voice, speed, and engine

System Requirements

  • OS: Windows 10/11, macOS 11+, or modern Linux (Ubuntu 20.04+)
  • RAM: 8 GB minimum, 16 GB recommended
  • Disk: 2 GB for the app; additional space for local models
  • GPU: Optional — NVIDIA GPU recommended for fast local image generation
  • Internet: Required only for the initial model download; the app itself runs offline
